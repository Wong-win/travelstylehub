<p><strong>About The Window King</strong><br />
The Window King is a French company belonging to the RIDORET group. The Ridoret group was founded by the Ridoret family who, from their very beginnings, worked in the <strong>joinery</strong> industry. The RIDORET group owns all its manufacturing plants in different French departments. The Window King holds quality certifications such as: Qualimarine, Cekal. Their credo? The choice of a short supply chain: from the factory to the customer.</p>
<p><strong>The services of The Window King</strong><br />
The product range offered by The Window King is focused on joinery equipment for the home. The offering extends from <strong>PVC</strong> or <strong>wood</strong> joinery, to the manufacture of sliding glass doors, shutters, French windows, as well as garage doors, gates, and entrance doors. All their products are custom-made and they have partnerships with other companies allowing them to offer showrooms to customers where the latter can come and see the finished products. <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-1358" src="http://travelstylehub.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Slide_r_seaux_sociaux_Le_Roi_de_la_Fen_tre-min-1024x369.jpg" alt="" width="1024" height="369" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Slide_r_seaux_sociaux_Le_Roi_de_la_Fen_tre-min-1024x369.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Slide_r_seaux_sociaux_Le_Roi_de_la_Fen_tre-min-300x108.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Slide_r_seaux_sociaux_Le_Roi_de_la_Fen_tre-min-768x277.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Slide_r_seaux_sociaux_Le_Roi_de_la_Fen_tre-min-960x346.jpg 960w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Slide_r_seaux_sociaux_Le_Roi_de_la_Fen_tre-min-1110x400.jpg 1110w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Slide_r_seaux_sociaux_Le_Roi_de_la_Fen_tre-min-585x211.jpg 585w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Slide_r_seaux_sociaux_Le_Roi_de_la_Fen_tre-min.jpg 1249w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></p>
