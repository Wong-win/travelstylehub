<h2 id="the-european-design-trail-why-spain-and-portugal-matter" class="mb-2 mt-4 font-display font-semimedium text-base first:mt-0 md:text-lg [hr+&amp;]:mt-4">The European Design Trail: Why Spain and Portugal Matter</h2>

<p class="my-2 [&amp;+p]:mt-4 [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:inline-block [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:pb-2">If you've ever wandered through Barcelona's Gràcia neighborhood or explored the artisan workshops tucked into Lisbon's Alfama district, you know there's something special about Iberian craftsmanship. These two countries have spent centuries perfecting the art of making beautiful, functional objects — from the azulejo tiles that adorn Portuguese facades to the sculptural furniture that defines Spanish modernism.</p>

<p class="my-2 [&amp;+p]:mt-4 [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:inline-block [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:pb-2">Cosyneo, a Charente-Maritime-based company on France's Atlantic coast, has built its entire business around this idea: that European manufacturing still means something. Rather than outsourcing production to the lowest bidder halfway across the world, they partner directly with <strong>family-owned workshops in Spain and Portugal</strong> — the kind of places where craftsmanship passes from one generation to the next, and where a perfectly fitted drawer or a flawlessly finished faucet is a matter of pride.</p>

<p class="my-2 [&amp;+p]:mt-4 [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:inline-block [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:pb-2">The result? Bathroom furniture that carries the weight of European design heritage — without the inflated price tag that usually comes with it.</p>

<p class="my-2 [&amp;+p]:mt-4 [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:inline-block [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:pb-2">Cosyneo throws out that entire playbook. The furniture goes from workshop to your doorstep — no showroom markup, no distributor fees, no hidden costs. Savings reach <strong>up to 45%</strong> compared to traditional retailers.</p>

<p class="my-2 [&amp;+p]:mt-4 [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:inline-block [&amp;_strong:has(+br)]:pb-2">"We follow every step of manufacturing closely, from material selection to the smallest finishing details," the Cosyneo team explains.
