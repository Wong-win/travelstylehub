## 2. What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for personal
information in order to read the site. When you contact us by email or
through a form, we receive the information you choose to send us: your name,
your email address, and the content of your message.

When you browse, our technology providers record technical information about
your visit: the pages you open, the pages that referred you to us, approximate
location derived from your IP address, the type of browser and device you
use, and the pages you were reading when the measurement stopped. This
information is used to understand which articles are read and to keep the
site working. It is not used to build a profile of you and it is not sold.

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file placed on your device when you visit a site. Some
are needed for the site to work and for audience measurement, and are always
active. The advertising cookies are optional: they are only loaded if you
agree to them through the cookie banner, which appears at the bottom of the
first page you open.

You can change your mind at any time through the **"Privacy choices"** link
at the bottom of every page. We also treat the **Do Not Track** and
**Global Privacy Control** signals as a refusal: in that case no banner is
shown and no optional tracker is loaded.

### 3.1 The trackers we actually use

| Name | Purpose | Provider | Type | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tsh_cookie_consent` | Remembers your cookie choice | Travel Style Hub | Local storage | 6 months |

These are the only trackers this website sets. The audience measurement
cookies `_ga` and `_ga_XXXXXX` are set on every visit; the affiliate trackers
are only set if you accept advertising.

### 3.2 Cookie-free audience measurement

Our host, Cloudflare, Inc., provides a cookie-free audience measurement tool
built into its network. It places no cookie, no identifier and nothing in
your browser's local storage, it uses a truncated form of your IP address, it
does not follow you from one site to another, and it cannot identify you. It
is limited to traffic statistics and is not used for advertising or
profiling. It is disclosed here for transparency.


### 3.3 Audience measurement is always active

Google Analytics 4 is loaded on every page and measures every visit. That
measurement does not depend on a choice you make in the cookie banner: the
banner only governs advertising. If you would rather not be measured, you can
block the analytics cookies in your browser settings or use the Do Not Track
signal, both of which we describe below.

The measurement is used to count visits and to understand which articles are
read. It is not used to build an advertising profile and the data is not sold.
This processing relies on our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is
used, balanced against your right to object, which you can exercise at any time
using the contact details in section 14.

is not affected. Affiliate links are marked with the technical attribute
`rel="sponsored"`, they are visually distinguished, and every article that
contains one carries an "affiliate link" notice at the top. We only work with
the affiliate networks listed in the table above, and the merchant receives

## 9. Your rights and choices

  Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act
  (PIPEDA), which applies to our commercial activity, you have the right to
  ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to ask us
  to correct it, and to withdraw your consent to its collection and use.

  Consent is required for the collection, use and disclosure of personal
  information that is not necessary to provide a service you have requested.
  We rely on your consent for audience measurement and for advertising
  cookies, and you can withdraw it at any time through the "Privacy
  choices" link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing consent does not
  affect the lawfulness of our use of the information before that point.

  You may also request access to and correction of your personal information
  under the privacy legislation of your province or territory of residence.
  If you live in Quebec, the Act respecting the protection of personal
  information in the private sector (Law 25) also gives you the right to
  withdraw consent, to request portability and to be informed of any
  automated decision. We respond to a verified request within 30 days, or
  within the period set by the law that applies to you.

  We do not sell personal information. You can ask us to delete the
  information we hold about you, and to stop contacting you for
  newsletters. To make a request, use the contact details in section 14.
