This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why,

with the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 2. What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for personal
information in order to read the site. When you contact us by email or
through a form, we receive the information you choose to send us: your name,
your email address, and the content of your message.

When you browse, our technology providers record technical information about
your visit: the pages you open, the pages that referred you to us, approximate
location derived from your IP address, the type of browser and device you
use, and the pages you were reading when the measurement stopped. This
information is used to understand which articles are read and to keep the
site working. It is not used to build a profile of you and it is not sold.

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file placed on your device when you visit a site. Some
are needed for the site to work and are always active. The rest are optional:
they are only loaded if you agree to them through the cookie banner, which
appears at the bottom of the first page you open.

You can change your mind at any time through the **"Privacy choices"** link
at the bottom of every page. We also treat the **Do Not Track** and
**Global Privacy Control** signals as a refusal: in that case no banner is
shown and no optional tracker is loaded.

### 3.1 The trackers we actually use

| Name | Purpose | Provider | Type | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tsh_cookie_consent` | Remembers your cookie choice | Travel Style Hub | Local storage | 6 months |

These are the only trackers this website sets. In particular, the audience
measurement cookies `_ga` and `_ga_XXXXXX` are only set if you accept audience
measurement, and the affiliate trackers are only set if you accept
advertising.

### 3.2 Cookie-free audience measurement

Our host, Cloudflare, Inc., provides a cookie-free audience measurement tool
built into its network. It places no cookie, no identifier and nothing in
your browser's local storage, it uses a truncated form of your IP address, it
does not follow you from one site to another, and it cannot identify you. It
is limited to traffic statistics and is not used for advertising or
profiling. It is disclosed here for transparency.

## 4. Affiliate links and advertising

Some articles contain affiliate links. If you follow one of them and buy
something, we may receive a commission from the merchant. The price you pay
is not affected. Affiliate links are marked with the technical attribute
`rel="sponsored"`, they are visually distinguished, and every article that
contains one carries an "affiliate link" notice at the top. We only work with
the affiliate networks listed in the table above, and the merchant receives
the information that the click-tracking link inherently carries.

Advertising banners are served by our affiliate networks. They are only
loaded if you accept advertising, and their presence is labelled
"Advertisement".

## 10. Your rights

## 10. Your rights

  Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection
  Act 2018, you have the right to:

  - be told what personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
  - have inaccurate information corrected;
  - have your information erased where we no longer need it;
  - restrict or object to how we use it;
  - receive it in a portable, machine-readable format;
  - withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of our use of it before that point.

  We respond within one month. We do not charge unless a request is
  manifestly unfounded or excessive. If you are unhappy with our response,
  you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

  Our legal basis is consent for audience measurement, for advertising
  cookies and for affiliate click tracking, all of them optional and all of
  them withdrawable; legitimate interests for keeping the site secure; and
  steps taken to enter into a contract for anything you send us.
